04 / A little background

Mathematician by training.
Builder by curiosity.

I’m an AI researcher and co-founder of Gosset. My work connects statistical methods with practical problems in human health.

Previously, I co-founded Saliency.ai, worked as a Director of AI at Clario, and conducted research in bioengineering and statistics at Stanford and in computer science at EPFL.

Along the way, I’ve worked on movement analysis, medical imaging, functional time series, and neural style transfer. The common thread is finding useful patterns in complex data, then building something people can use.
