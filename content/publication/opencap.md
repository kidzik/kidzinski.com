OpenCap combines video-based pose estimation, machine learning, and biomechanical simulation to estimate human movement and musculoskeletal forces from two or more smartphone cameras.

Scott D. Uhlrich, Antoine Falisse, and Lukas Kidzinski contributed equally to this work.

Read the [published paper](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011462) for the methods, validation, and study limitations.
