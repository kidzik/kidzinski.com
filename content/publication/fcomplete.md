We use matrix completion to estimate smooth individual trajectories from sparse and irregular longitudinal measurements. The framework also supports multivariate measurements and regression.

Published in *Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics*, **33**(2), 551–566 (2024), following online publication on November 15, 2023. [Read the published paper](https://doi.org/10.1080/10618600.2023.2257257).

Earlier versions circulated as *Longitudinal data analysis using matrix completion*. The [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.08771) and [fcomplete package](https://github.com/kidzik/fcomplete) remain available.
