# Journal marks

These small identifiers sit beside the full journal name. Images are stored locally so visitors do not contact publisher image servers.

| Local asset | Official source | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `nature.png` | [Nature small-format mark](https://www.nature.com/static/images/favicons/nature/favicon-32x32-3fe59ece92.png) | Nature Medicine, Nature Communications, npj Digital Medicine, Scientific Reports |
| `plos-computational-biology.png` | [PLOS Computational Biology wordmark](https://journals.plos.org/resource/img/comp-biol/logo.png) | PLOS Computational Biology |
| `jcgs.gif` | [American Statistical Association journal listing](https://www.amstat.org/publications/journals) | Official JCGS cover badge |
| `jrssb.png` | [Royal Statistical Society Series B page](https://rss.org.uk/news-publication/publications/journals/series-b/) | Official Series B cover badge |

The journal name remains visible text; decorative images use empty alternative text to avoid duplicate screen-reader announcements. Entries without a supplied asset retain their text label.
