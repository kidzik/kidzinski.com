# Publication archive sources

Checked against [Lukas Kidzinski’s Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=7rvNQJoAAAAJ&hl=en&pagesize=100) on October 15, 2026. Both profile pages were read (144 Scholar rows). The website now contains 45 distinct records, compared with 15 before this update.

The expansion covers the distinct papers with at least ten citations in the retrieved Scholar list, together with the existing archive, the recent Gosset preprint, the functional time-series survey, and the cellular classification paper. Scholar citation counts are used only to identify omissions; they are not displayed or automatically synchronized. Duplicate citations, superseded preprints, conference abstracts superseded by full papers, patents, software packages, theses, and container/book records whose listed authors do not include Kidzinski are excluded.

Titles, full author lists, venues, and links were checked against publisher-deposited Crossref records, the indexed author manuscripts in Europe PMC, official proceedings, and arXiv metadata. Only Lukas’s display name is standardized to the requested spelling. Published versions replace preprint-only records where identified. Statistics journals and books use their issue/print year where it differs from the first online date (for example, the prosthetics chapter appeared online in 2019 and in the 2020 book). Existing routes are retained.

The five homepage selections and their content are retained. Archive-only long author lists use a native expandable disclosure; every author remains in the searchable HTML and on the paper’s detail page.
